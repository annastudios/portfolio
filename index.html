Askive — Chat with your documents

Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file and ask it questions. Every answer cites the exact passages it came from. Built solo in 3 weeks.

Goal
Chat with your own documents, and be able to trust the answers.
Built
FastAPI + ChromaDB retrieval. Gemini answers only from the retrieved passages.
Hurdle
Queries took 5+ minutes, and uploads kept hitting embedding rate limits.
Fix
Moved the blocking LLM call off FastAPI's event loop: under 10 s per query. Batched all embeddings into one request: no more rate-limit failures.
